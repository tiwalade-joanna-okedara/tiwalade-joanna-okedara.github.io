The spec is the product

Each product has one guide file that the AI reads before touching anything. A few of the rules from startbuddi’s:

Language rules

Say “contacts”, not CRM. “Send a bill”, not invoice. “Set up”, not configure. A 16-year-old should understand every label.

AI rules

Never fire an action without a confirm step. Keep replies to a few sentences, end with one action or one question, and never make up names or numbers.

Ship rules

One branch. The typecheck has to pass before a push. A failed build never goes live.

Honesty rules

Sample data is labelled as sample. AI output is labelled as a draft.
